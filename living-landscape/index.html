Phase One Digital Prototype

Technology that brings guests closer to the land.

Noualla Living Landscape is a simple mobile companion connecting regenerative hospitality, ecological learning and community-led experiences. It is designed to support human hospitality — not replace it.

How It Works

One quiet digital layer. Three practical functions.

01

Explore the Land

Guests access stories through an interactive map and discreet QR points located around the property. Each stop explains part of the landscape, its restoration and the knowledge connected to it.

02

Support Local Experiences

Guests can discover planned pottery, cooking, craft and land-based activities and express interest before or during their stay.

03

Follow Verified Impact

Noualla will progressively publish environmental and community indicators using measured information rather than generic sustainability claims.

Guests may use the companion when it adds value, and put their phones away when it does not.

Prototype Trail

Every part of the land has a story.

This prototype demonstrates the first three trail points. The operational Phase One trail is planned to include six to eight locations across the property.

From Degraded Hillside to Living Landscape

Restoration began in 2022, before the hospitality concept was developed. More than 600 trees have since been planted across a 9,300 m² hillside, alongside terraces, planting areas and long-term land care.

Water on the Slope

Terraces, swales, irrigation systems and greywater reuse help slow water, reduce erosion and support vegetation in a dry Mediterranean landscape.

Habitat for Pollinators

Trees, flowering plants and chemical-conscious land care are creating more habitat for bees, butterflies and other pollinators. Future biodiversity observations will be recorded through the Living Landscape system.

Beyond One Property

Designed for Noualla. Adaptable to other rural destinations.

Noualla's physical retreat will remain intentionally small, growing from four initial units to a maximum of eight. The part designed to scale is the framework around the stay.

Restoration-First Development

A practical sequence that begins with land care before hospitality expansion.

Community Experience Framework

Simple principles for developing activities with local providers and sharing tourism value responsibly.

Impact Measurement

A manageable set of environmental and community indicators for small rural projects.

Digital Toolkit

Mobile interpretation, local experience access and transparent reporting without requiring a complex native application.

After being tested at Noualla, the framework can be documented and adapted by other rural accommodations and community tourism initiatives.
