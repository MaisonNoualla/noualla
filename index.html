A Place Returning to Life

From forgotten land to a living landscape.

Noualla began with a simple idea: that damaged land can heal, and that hospitality can become a force for regeneration.

Over years of restoring soil, planting trees and rebuilding natural systems, this hillside has slowly transformed into a living ecosystem.

Northern Morocco · Mediterranean Hills · 9,300 m² of Restored Land

The Noualla Experience

A future rooted in simplicity

Noualla will welcome guests into a slower rhythm — shaped by nature, traditional knowledge and meaningful encounters.

Future experiences will include —

Women-led cooking experiences

Discover Moroccan cuisine through intimate meals prepared with local ingredients and traditional knowledge.

Women-led pottery workshops

Learn from local artisans and experience the craft traditions that connect generations.

Farm walks

Explore the gardens, terraces and ecosystems that make the land come alive.

Seasonal harvest experiences

Participate in planting, harvesting and the rhythms of agricultural life.

Nature & night experiences

Stargazing, quiet walks and discovering the hidden life of the Mediterranean hills.
