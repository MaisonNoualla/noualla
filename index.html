A regenerative retreat growing from the hills of Northern Morocco.

A Place Returning to Life

From forgotten land to a living landscape.

Noualla began with a simple idea: that damaged land can heal, and that hospitality can become a force for regeneration.

Over years of restoring soil, planting trees and rebuilding natural systems, this hillside has slowly transformed into a living ecosystem.

Northern Morocco · Mediterranean Hills · 9,300 m² of Restored Land

Currently in Development

A place taking shape slowly

Noualla is intentionally being built step by step. Every tree planted, every terrace restored and every detail created brings the vision closer to life.

Already in Place

  • 9,300 m² of privately owned land
  • Restoration active since 2022
  • More than 600 trees planted
  • One permanent local land-stewardship role
  • Noualla brand and website established
  • Living Landscape prototype developed

Currently in Development

  • Required regulatory and technical processes
  • Architectural and infrastructure planning
  • First four accommodation units
  • Community experience collaborations
  • Phase One impact-measurement system

Long-Term Direction

  • Maximum of eight accommodation units
  • Expanded environmental measurement
  • More local employment and purchasing
  • A documented toolkit for other rural destinations

Noualla will scale by proving and sharing the model — not by overcrowding the land.
