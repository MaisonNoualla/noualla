OUR STORY

A forgotten hillside.
A new future.

Noualla begins with a simple belief: the future of tourism lies in restoring places rather than consuming them. On a quiet hillside overlooking the Rif Mountains, we are transforming a piece of land into a living example of regenerative hospitality rooted in Moroccan culture. Every tree planted, every terrace restored and every wall built carries the same purpose: to leave the land healthier than we found it.

FIVE PILLARS

Regeneration

Restoring ecosystems through permaculture and responsible land stewardship.

Hospitality

Small-scale experiences designed around authenticity rather than mass tourism.

Craftsmanship

Celebrating Moroccan artisans and traditional building techniques.

Nature

Living among orchards, gardens, wildlife and mountain landscapes.

Community

Creating opportunities that benefit neighbouring families and local culture.

THE LAND

Where mountains meet the Mediterranean.

Located in northern Morocco, Noualla sits between Tetouan, Chefchaouen and the Mediterranean coast. The project combines regenerative agriculture, traditional architecture and slow travel to create a place that feels deeply rooted in its landscape.

CURRENT STATUS

Currently in development.

Noualla is being carefully developed over the coming years. Rather than building quickly, we are restoring the land step by step with patience, craftsmanship and respect for nature. Follow our journey as every season brings new trees, new gardens and new spaces to life.
